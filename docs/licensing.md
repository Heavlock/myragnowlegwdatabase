## Why this license

[PolyForm Noncommercial](https://polyformproject.org/licenses/noncommercial/1.0.0) is a standardized software license designed for noncommercial use. Its express provision for separate licenses lets the author grant commercial rights independently.

MIT, Apache, and GPL licenses do not meet the project's commercial restriction: open-source licenses must allow business use under the [Open Source Definition](https://opensource.org/osd). Accordingly, myrag is described as source available. Creative Commons also [recommends software-specific licenses](https://creativecommons.org/faq/#can-i-apply-a-creative-commons-license-to-software) instead of CC licenses for software.

The repository includes the license at its root, following [GitHub's licensing guidance](https://docs.github.com/en/repositories/managing-your-repositorys-settings-and-features/customizing-your-repository/licensing-a-repository). The upstream text is available in the [PolyForm license repository](https://github.com/polyformproject/polyform-licenses/blob/1.0.0/PolyForm-Noncommercial-1.0.0.md). The SPDX identifier is `PolyForm-Noncommercial-1.0.0`.
