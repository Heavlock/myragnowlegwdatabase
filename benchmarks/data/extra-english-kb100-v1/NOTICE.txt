EXTRA-English-KB100 v1

Original, synthetic English benchmark authored for the myragnowlegwdatabase project.
Aster Works and all its procedures, people, requirements and operational details
in this dataset are fictional. These articles are test fixtures, not advice or
instructions for a real organization. No Wikipedia text is included.

The same assistant authored the documents, questions and relevance labels.
Labels were checked against supporting passages and related distractors, but
have not been independently reviewed by humans. This controlled, small-corpus
test supplements the public SciFact benchmark; it does not replace it.

Each of the 100 articles has three queries: direct, paraphrase, and scenario.
The scenario questions introduce constraints to distinguish neighboring workflows.
Twenty additional multi-article questions each have two relevant targets.
All 320 queries are evaluated against the complete corpus. Relevance means the
article supplies the requested procedure or fact; topical overlap alone is not
relevance. Each query has authored targets and supporting passages.

Freeze policy: source records, corpus, queries, qrels, and judgments are hashed
before any model run. No post-result edits or question filtering are permitted
within this version. A changed dataset requires a new version.
